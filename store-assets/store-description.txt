Calm Sky is a light, airy Chrome theme for quiet, unhurried browsing. A soft sky-blue window frame wraps pale blue-grey tabs, a near-white toolbar sits under them, and the new-tab page opens onto a calm, washed-blue expanse. Deep slate text keeps every label and icon crisp on all of those light surfaces. The design is flat colour throughout - no wallpaper, no patterns, no gradients - and the icon distills it into a sun resting on a still horizon.
